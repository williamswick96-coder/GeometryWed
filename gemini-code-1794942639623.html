Core relationships for Lessons 3.7, 3.8, 5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 5.4, and 5.6:

  • Slope Formula: $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$. Nonvertical lines are perpendicular iff $m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1$.
  • Midpoint Formula: $M = \left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2},\; \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)$.
  • Distance Formula: $d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$.
  • Trapezoid Area: $A = \frac{1}{2}(b_1 + b_2)h$, where $b_1, b_2$ are the parallel bases and $h$ is the perpendicular altitude.
  • Circle & Square Areas: $A_{\text{circle}} = \pi r^2$ and $A_{\text{square}} = s^2$.
